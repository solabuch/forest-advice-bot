Chanterelles near the anthill

Dear neighbours from the «Біля мурашника» mitten! I am a hedgehog from the forest across the river, and I found a good mushroom spot near your home.

- Chanterelles grow under the old spruce, 50 steps west of the anthill.
- Pick them two days after rain, not earlier: young ones are too small.
- Do not pick a mushroom with a ring on its stem — it may be poisonous.

Good luck and full baskets!
